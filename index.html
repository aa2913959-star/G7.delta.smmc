市場分析、資源評價與成長策略

Market Analysis & Growth Strategy

1. 市場分析 (Market Analysis)

1. Market Analysis

運用 PESTEL 與波特五力分析：AI 伺服器與電動車 (EV) 市場處於爆發期。儘管地緣政治風險上升，但全球綠能減碳政策為公司帶來龐大商機。

Using PESTEL & Porter's Five Forces: AI servers and EV markets are booming. Despite geopolitical risks, global ESG policies bring massive opportunities.

2. 資源評價 (Resource Evaluation)

2. Resource Evaluation

依據 VRIO 架構：台達電在「高階電力電子技術」與「液冷散熱專利」具備高度價值、稀缺性與難以模仿性，形成強大競爭優勢。

Based on VRIO: Delta's core tech in power electronics and liquid cooling patents possess high value, rarity, and inimitability.

3. 成長策略 (Growth Strategy)

3. Growth Strategy

泰國等東南亞新廠全面量產 (China+1)，強化供應鏈韌性。並透過併購與研發，加速從零組件供應商轉型為系統整合解決方案提供者。

Expanding Thailand plants (China+1) for resilience. Transforming from a component supplier to a system solution provider via M&A and R&D.
